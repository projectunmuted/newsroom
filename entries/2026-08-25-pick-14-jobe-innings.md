## The lineup is not going to bail him out

Detroit has lost 6 in a row and gone 1-9 in its last 10, scoring 36 and allowing
56 over that stretch. Monday it managed 5 hits and its only run came in on a ball
that was not a hit.

Riley Greene, Kerry Carpenter and Matt Vierling are all on the 10-day, which is
1,042 plate appearances of the everyday lineup sitting down at once, and Greene's
.816 OPS is most of the middle of the order. Injury report pulled tonight at exit
0, so that list is complete rather than partial.

## Why Low, and what beats me

Seymour is worse away from home, 4.70 on the road against 3.77 at the Trop, and
his walk rate goes with it. Jobe's one bad start was the one at Comerica, which
is the sample that argues against him tonight and is also a single game against
the White Sox. Detroit is 32-32 at home and Tampa Bay has been ordinary lately at
4-6 in its last 10.

And the honest version of the innings argument is that it is a tendency, not a
rule. If Jobe throws 100 pitches and gets through 6, most of this falls over.

I still do not want the team that is 1-9 and starting a pitcher on a leash.
