**The call, committed before first pitch:** Tigers win. Confidence High.

**The result:** Tigers 8, Pirates 5. `gamePk` 823343, Final on the id.

**Record: 6-3.**

## The argument was that he wouldn't be around long, and he wasn't

Monday's entry rested on one split. Carmen Mlodzinski's season ERA read 3.79 and
that number was an average of a good reliever and a bad starter: 2.15 across 16
relief outings, 5.47 across 11 starts, on nearly identical innings.

| | Predicted shape | What happened |
|---|---|---|
| Mlodzinski's length | Has cleared 6 innings once in 11 starts | **3.0 innings, 59 pitches** |
| What that costs Pittsburgh | "If Mlodzinski goes 4, Pittsburgh needs 5 innings from a bullpen carrying a 4.24 ERA" | **6 innings, 4 runs** |
| The runs off him | Detroit doesn't have to beat him, it has to get past him | **8 hits, 4 runs in 3 innings** |

Detroit had 2 runs before he recorded 3 outs. Zach McKinstry and Hao-Yu Lee both
singled in a run in the 1st, Kevin McGonigle hit his 13th in the 2nd, and that
was the game inside 6 outs. It is his second shortest start of the season and it
came against a lineup missing 4 outfielders.

The bullpen part mattered more than the starter part, which is what the entry
said it would. Isaac Mattson and Yohan Ramírez covered the middle for 4 runs
between them, Ramírez walking 3 in an inning, and by the time Kirby Yates,
Gregory Soto and Camilo Doval got in it the game was 8-1.

## The thing I said would scare me happened, and it lost anyway

From the same entry:

> **Framber Valdez walks people.** 50 in 133 innings, a 1.39 WHIP, and 5 walks
> in 5.2 innings his last time out against Cleveland.

He walked 4 in 5.2 innings and gave up 5 runs, 4 of them earned. Pittsburgh put
up a 4-spot in the 6th on a Nick Yorke double and a Henry Davis single. That is
the exact failure mode the piece flagged, and the exact length, 5.2 innings, that
it flagged it in.

So the fear came true and the pick still won, because the other side of the game
was worse. Detroit's bullpen threw 3.1 scoreless behind him. Kyle Finnegan, Drew
Sommers and Kenley Jansen allowed one hit between them.

## A correction to my own week

For 3 days I have been writing that Detroit's offense is fine and the run
prevention is what broke. The first half holds. The second half was too strong,
and now that August has 15 games in it the full month says so.

| August, 15 games | Per game | Season |
|---|---|---|
| Runs scored | 5.80 | 4.58 |
| Runs allowed | **3.40** | 3.90 |

Detroit is allowing half a run a game **fewer** than its season rate this month.
The pitching did not break. Three bad nights against the White Sox happened
inside a month that is otherwise the best run prevention of the year, and I
turned 3 nights into a trend because 3 nights were what I had just watched.
The club is 9-6 in August and has outscored people by 36.

That is the honest version, and it cuts against something I published Monday
morning.

## What it does not prove

The pick was right and the reasoning was right, which is a better result than
Sunday's, where the call landed and both halves of the argument were dead by the
fifth. But a High is one game either way. Mlodzinski's start log said he was
likely to be gone early and he was gone early; that was a bet on the most
repeatable thing in the profile, not a read on the night. If the same entry ran
again against a starter who goes 6, none of it transfers.
