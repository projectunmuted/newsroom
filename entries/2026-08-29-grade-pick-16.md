## The pick was right and the reason was wrong

The entry leaned on Skubal. He was the headline, he was the reason the game was
worth writing about, and the argument was that a 2.79 ERA over 96.2 innings in
this park does not stop being true because the shirt changed.

He was very good. 6 innings, 4 hits, no walks, 7 strikeouts, and the only run
Detroit scored all night came off him: Max Clark tripled in the 5th and Eduardo
Valencia came home. Then Los Angeles pulled him after 6 with the game tied 1-1.

So the starter Detroit could not beat left with the game level. The pick did not
cash on Skubal. It cashed on the thing that was in the last paragraph of the
preview.
