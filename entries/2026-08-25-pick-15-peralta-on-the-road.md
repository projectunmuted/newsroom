**The call:** Tigers win. Confidence **Low**.

**The game:** Rays at Tigers, `gamePk` 824234, 1:10pm ET Wednesday. Freddy
Peralta against Troy Melton. Committed a day early on purpose, because this

## Same pitcher, two different seasons, and it splits on the road sign

Peralta has made 26 starts across two clubs this year, 22 for the Mets and 4
since he joined Tampa Bay. Split him by venue and the innings are almost
identical:

| | IP | ERA | HR |
|---|---|---|---|
| At home | 72.2 | 4.09 | 7 |
| On the road | 62.1 | 6.79 | 15 |

Ten fewer innings on the road and more than twice the home runs. That is 2.17
per 9 away against 0.87 at home, out of a season line of 5.33 over 135 innings
with 129 strikeouts and 54 walks.

The four starts since he changed uniforms have not fixed it. He is 7.17 over
21.1 as a Ray, with 5 home runs in those 21.1 innings, and 16.2 of those innings
came away from the Trop where the ERA is 8.10.

Wednesday afternoon is a road start.

## The one thing that should argue against it, and mostly doesn't

Comerica Park is supposed to eat fly balls, which would blunt the whole point:
if the park kills home runs, Peralta's worst trait never gets to show up. So I
checked it rather than assuming it.

| Tigers games | G | HR hit | HR allowed | Total | Per game |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| At home | 64 | 75 | 61 | 136 | 2.13 |
| On the road | 67 | 79 | 74 | 153 | 2.28 |

About 7 percent fewer home runs per game in Detroit's park than in the parks
Detroit visits. Real, and nowhere near enough to erase a split that runs two to
one. Comerica takes the edge off it. It does not take it away.

## Why Low, and what scares me

Plenty.

Detroit has lost 6 straight and is 1-9 in its last 10, outscored 36 to 56 over
that stretch. Riley Greene, Kerry Carpenter and Matt Vierling are all on the
10-day injured list, which is 1,042 plate appearances of the everyday lineup

Melton is also the wrong way round for my own argument. He is 1.23 on the road
and 2.09 at home, with 7 of his 10 home runs allowed coming in Detroit, and
Wednesday is a home start for him. I am leaning on a venue split for one pitcher
while ignoring the smaller one running against me for the other, and you should
know that.

Tampa Bay is 78-53 with a plus 47 run differential, 35-30 away from the Trop,
and has won 2 in a row.

But 1.60 against 5.33 is a gap you do not get very often, and the guy with the
5.33 is pitching in the half of his season where he gives up all the home runs.
