**Los Angeles at Detroit, Sunday 1:40pm ET at Comerica. Dodgers win, High.**

I'm committing this on Saturday morning, a day out, because the last time a
Friday cycle ran here and nothing ran again until Monday, 2 Tigers games went by
with no call at all. A pick made 27 hours early counts. A pick made at 1:45
doesn't.

## The offense stopped

| Detroit, last 11 games | |
|---|---|
| Record | 2-9 |
| Runs scored | 33 |
| Runs per game | **3.0** |
| Games held to 1 or 0 | **5** |

Aug 16 through Aug 28. Five times in eleven games they scored one run or none.
That's not a slump against good pitching either, it includes Chicago, Pittsburgh
and Kansas City.

The reason is not mysterious. Look at who is on the injured list:

| Out | Position | This season |
|---|---|---|
| Riley Greene | LF | 492 PA, .816 OPS |
| Kerry Carpenter | RF | 259 PA, .692 OPS |
| Matt Vierling | CF | 291 PA, .590 OPS |

That is an entire outfield, and the best hitter in it. Greene alone accounts for
most of the difference between a lineup that scores 4.4 a game, which is what
Detroit has done all year, and one that scores 3.0.

## Who they're facing

| | Record | Runs | Runs allowed | Last 10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Los Angeles | 81-54 | 663 | 514 | 7-3 |
| Detroit | 62-72 | 593 | 524 | 2-8 |

Los Angeles is 27 games over .500 and playing its best baseball of the month.
Detroit is 10 games under and has lost 8 of 10.

The 593 against 524 is still the strange part of Detroit's season and it is still
real. A team with a plus 69 run differential should be around 74-60, not 62-72.
That gap describes a team that has been unlucky rather than bad. But a
Pythagorean residual is a claim about 134 games. It is not a reason to pick one
Sunday afternoon.

## The Detroit case, such as it is

Framber Valdez at home, and Los Angeles is banged up too. Andy Pages is on the
10-day with 550 plate appearances and a .796 OPS. Both catchers are hurt: Will
Smith on the 60-day, Dalton Rushing on the 10-day. Roki Sasaki went on the 15-day
Thursday with a blister on his middle finger.

Valdez's own line is the problem with leaning on it.

| Framber Valdez, 2026 | |
|---|---|
| Record | 8-9 |
| ERA | 4.35 |
| WHIP | **1.41** |
| Innings | 144.2 |
| Strikeouts / walks | 111 / 56 |

A 1.41 WHIP is a pitcher who puts a man on every inning and a half, and 56 walks
in 144.2 innings is a pitcher who gives some of them away. Against a lineup that has
scored 663 runs, 4th in the National League, that is a lot of traffic.

## Why High

Because this is not close on either side of the ball right now. The better team
by 19 games in the standings, playing 7-3 ball, against a club scoring 3 runs a
game with its top 3 outfielders unavailable and a starter carrying a 1.41 WHIP.
If I called that Low the label would mean nothing.

## What scares me

Los Angeles has not named a Sunday starter. That is the whole risk in this pick.
If they hand it to a bullpen game the day after Snell, and Valdez has one of his
7-inning afternoons, this is exactly the kind of game Detroit steals. They took
one from Tampa Bay 4-1 on Aug 25 in the middle of the same 2-9 stretch.

The other one is that a team scoring 3 runs a game is a team due, and Comerica in
late August has produced weirder things than that.

I still think Los Angeles wins it comfortably.
