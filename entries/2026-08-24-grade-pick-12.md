the best arm in the series. Here is what each of them did:

| | What the pick said | Friday night |
|---|---|---|

from 1.84 runs to 1.46 in a single start. Melton's season ERA went from 1.49 to
1.60. The entry called the split a tiebreaker rather than a thesis, and said so
in print, which is the only part of it that survived the night.

## The way Kansas City scored is the part worth keeping

They scored in the 4th, the 5th, the 6th, the 7th and the 8th. One run each
time. 5 innings, 5 runs, and not once did they put 2 on the board in the same
frame.

Detroit's 2 came together in the 7th, off John Schreiber, and by then it was
4-0. Tyler Holton and Ty Madden each gave one back in the innings on either side
of it.

That is the shape of a lot of these. A team that never has a big inning against
you is a team you have to beat with your own bullpen holding, and Detroit's has
been converting 26 of 54 all season.
